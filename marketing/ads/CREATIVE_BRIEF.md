# Richy — Two 3D Launch Ads (Blender)

**Started:** 27 Aug 2026 · **Format:** 1080×1920 vertical, 30fps, ~20s each ·
**Tone:** premium calm — "expensive, warm, understated wealth" · **Language:** English (Hebrew later)

Reference: the Advisor screen (Cornflower Ocean theme) — cream field, EB Garamond serif,
dark-chocolate R monogram in gold, periwinkle ribbons, white pill chips.
Render: Blender 5.2 Eevee at 720×1280, AI-upscaled to 1080×1920.
UI cards are browser-rendered PNGs (pixel-perfect EB Garamond) textured onto planes.

**Positioning (Alon, 27 Aug):** not just another budgeting diary — the only service you need
to actually stop impulse buys and build financial freedom, with Richard, your AI financial
personal assistant. Business + investing accounts shown as the #1 format to track investments
and run your business with Richard's help.

**Legal rails (locked — "track + business coach" framing):**
- Investing = *tracking*, "one clean view." Richard NEVER picks, recommends, or comments on
  holdings. No tickers or named securities anywhere in frame — asset-class rows only.
- Compliance strip on screen ≥3s in each ad, verbatim from the app:
  *"Richard is an AI assistant, not a licensed financial advisor. Always do your own research
  before making money decisions."*
- No bank logos, no "connects to your bank," no fabricated precise figures presented as real.
