# Richy — Ad 3 draft: "The Homework"

**Format:** 1080×1920 vertical, 30fps, ~14.0s (420 frames) · organic short-form (TikTok/Reels/Shorts)
**Where it came from:** you trying to save but everything leaking and feeling complicated — landed on
"never knowing where it went" + "budgeting always felt like homework," not a single quantified stat.
**Positioning:** Richy isn't another app asking you to do more work — it does the boring, complicated
part for you.
**Feeling target:** relief — easier than you thought, delivered in two stages (screen resolves it,
then Richard names what he did).

## Beat sheet

| Beat | Time | Frames | Scene | VO / on-screen |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.0–3.5s | 0–105 | Clutter: receipts, spreadsheet cells, category dropdowns, statement PDFs, tumbling loosely in 3D space. Slightly overwhelming, not panicked — the "I'll deal with this eventually" pile. | "You were going to sort this weekend." |
| 2 | 3.5–6.5s | 105–195 | Camera pushes in. Every scattered piece sweeps toward center and resolves into a single Richy card materializing. No VO — let the visual carry the relief. | (silent / soft whoosh) |
| 3 | 6.5–11.0s | 195–330 | The clean card sits alone: one total, clear categories, calm. Richard's bubble slides in beside it. | "I sorted the last 43 charges. You don't have to." |
| 4 | 11.0–14.0s | 330–420 | Wordmark + tagline. Compliance strip fades in at frame 330, holds to 420 — exactly 3.0s, the floor. | "Richy. One less thing." |

## Notes before building
- "43 charges" is a placeholder — swap for a number you'd actually stand behind. Specific beats round.
- Keep this consistent with statements the user uploads, not a live bank feed — same rule as the rest
  of the campaign, and this concept implies transaction-level visibility so it's worth being careful here.
- Real Richy UI/cards where they fit (per your call); 3D treatment only where it adds something native
  screenshots can't — the clutter-to-order sweep in beat 2 is the one moment that earns it.

## Rails, unchanged from Ad 1 / Ad 2
- Cornflower Ocean palette (cream / periwinkle / gold), EB Garamond
- No bank/fintech logos, no "connects automatically" claim
- Compliance strip verbatim, on screen ≥3s:
  "Richard is an AI assistant, not a licensed financial advisor. Always do your own research before
  making money decisions."
