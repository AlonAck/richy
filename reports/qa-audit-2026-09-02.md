## 3. Richard Watch — the flagship feature has never been QA'd

Shipped 30 August, sits on the Overview hero, is the least-finished code in the app. All anchors verified directly.

### 3.1 The header on all four screens renders `watchBrief` — CRITICAL, one-line fix
The header title chain (`:33779`) ends in `: tr(currentTab)`. `tr()` returns the key itself when unmatched (`:1355`, `|| key`). `watchBrief`, `watchGoal`, `watchForecast`, `watchOuts` are **in no dictionary**. So the sticky header on every Watch screen reads the literal camelCase string **`watchBrief`** — in *every* language, English included. It is the first thing a user sees after tapping the hero.

### 3.2 Zero translated strings in the entire feature — HIGH
`awk 'NR>=11960 && NR<=12624' | grep -c 'tr('` → **0**. Every string is a hardcoded English literal. The app sets `dir="rtl"` for `he`/`ar` (`:654`), so a Hebrew user gets four RTL-laid-out screens of English prose under a header reading `watchBrief`.

**The discipline existed and was skipped selectively:** the same commit added `fmDraftRefund` to all seven dictionaries (`:865`, `:1017`, `:1169`, `:1321`). The FoundMoney *button* was localised; the four new *screens* were not.

### 3.3 Nothing in Richard Watch can be dismissed, snoozed or muted — HIGH
`RW_ACTIONS` (`:11691`) defines `dismiss`, `snooze`, `remindBefore`. `rwActionsFor` (`:11912`) attaches them to every signal. **`.actions` appears exactly once in 33,734 lines** — at the construction site (`:11768`). It is never read.

A cash-cliff or budget-pace warning therefore pins itself to the top of the Daily Brief in the red band **for the rest of the calendar month**, with no way to acknowledge it or tell Richard "I know — it's the wedding."

### 3.4 `WatchOuts` is a shipped screen that cannot be reached — MEDIUM
`grep -n watchOuts` returns **exactly one line**: the render guard at `:33863`. Same for `buildWatchDigest`, `watchPromptPayload`, `WatchBackLink` — one occurrence each. The digest system, whose own comment (`:11891`) promises *"a user is told about a leak once, not every morning until they act,"* is entirely dead. That absence is visible: the Daily Brief headline (`:12237`) says `watch.leaks.length + " new leak"` where `watch.leaks` is **every open leak**. On day 40 it still says "12 new leaks."

### 3.5 Richard offers to cancel your car insurance to fund a goal — HIGH
Every detector is fed `leakable` (`:11101`, `!g.essential`). `detectAnnualRenewals(tx, cats)` (`:11145`) is fed **raw `tx`** — the only one that bypasses the filter. The `ESSENTIAL_HINTS` comment (`:11231`) states the exact failure it exists to prevent: *"the goal planner answers 'you are 54/mo short' with 'cancel your rent'."*

A ₪4,800 annual car-insurance premium → monthlyised to ₪400/mo → sorts to the **top** of the greedy pool → the goal screen says *"Cancelling 1 thing covers it"* with insurance as the checked row and **"Cancel both"** as the primary button. Same for property tax and tuition. Fee rows also qualify, so the plan can tell the user to "cancel" their *overdraft fees*.

### 3.6 Two chevron rows that look like navigation but permanently rewrite the goal — HIGH
`giveOneMoreMonth` (`:12365`) and `aimForReal` (`:12380`) render as `<button>` cards ending in a `chevron` (`:12433`, `:12441`) — identical to every "go here" row in the app. One tap fires `onSaveGoals(...)`. No confirm, no undo, no record of the old value.

`aimForReal` sets the target to `saved + (actualPerMonth * monthsLeft)` where `actualPerMonth` is the **trailing 3-month average net** — negative for exactly the overspending users this screen exists for. The card advertises *"Aim for **-₪1,200**"* and one tap sets a negative savings target. On an overdue goal (`monthsLeft === 0`) it silently marks the goal complete at whatever is in it.

### 3.7 The forward-looking engine is blind to Hebrew merchant names — HIGH
```js
// fmNormLabel (:11185) — keeps Hebrew, deliberately:
.replace(/[^a-z0-9֐-׿؀-ۿ]+/g, " ")
// normalizeMerchant (:10863) — strips it:
s = s.replace(/[^a-z0-9&]+/g, " ");
```
A transaction labelled `נטפליקס` normalises to `""` and `groupByMerchant` drops it (`if (!key) return;`, `:10895`). Everything forward-looking routes through it: `forecastUpcoming`, `detectAnnualRenewals`, and the cash-cliff `committed` figure. For a Hebrew-entering user — the primary market — *Next 30 Days* is permanently "Nothing on the horizon yet.", no renewal is ever warned about, and the cash cliff always computes with `committed = 0`.

### 3.8 Five window/scope errors — HIGH
- **Cash cliff compares month-to-date income against full-month projected expense** (`var projected = m.expense + committed;`, `:11585`). Upcoming *expenses* are forecast; upcoming *income* is structurally invisible. Anyone paid on the 28th who logs any small income earlier gets *"This month ends ₪7,400 short"* in the red band **every month from the 1st to payday**, then it clears itself, having been wrong throughout. `detectSavingsSlip` (`:11598`) has the identical asymmetry.
- **Unclamped percentages in body copy** (`nowPct`, `:11649`, rendered `:11836`). ₪60 income against ₪900 spend reads *"-1400% this month against a 24% average."* `detectGoalRisk` clamps its own pct (`:11589`); this one doesn't.
- **Per-year rows under a per-month headline, same screen.** Headline: ₪400/mo Recoverable (`:12246`). Rows below: "₪2,400/yr" (`:12045`). Nothing states the 12× relationship. The hero then shows `recoverableAnnual` as "potential to protect each year" (`:10251`) — the same number at three magnitudes across two screens one tap apart.
- **Fees that stopped years ago are annualised as recoverable forever** (`:11323`). `span` counts *months in which a fee occurred*, not months elapsed, and `detectFees` has **no recency window**. Six overdraft fees across two months in 2024 → ₪1,260/yr still headlined as recoverable in 2026. Both its siblings have windows (`:11406`, `:11348`).
- **The goal-rescue plan double-counts** (`:11659`). `linkLeaksToGap` ignores `subsumedBy` — the rule that `recoverableTotal()`, `rwSumMonthly()` and `PickCancelSheet` (`:12468`) all honour. Netflix appears twice under different names, the total inflates, and it mis-routes the "we can fix this" vs "I can't close this" branch.

### 3.9 Smaller but visible
- **The busy Daily Brief has no back link.** The *quiet* branch opens with `SubViewBack` (`:12193`); the *busy* branch — what most users see — has none, and `watchBrief` isn't in `MAIN_TABS_SET` (`:32948`), so the tab bar renders with nothing lit.
- **A 40px-wide column for a 10-character ISO date** (`:12569`, at 11px). Every row in Next 30 Days wraps.
- **A CSS gradient passed as an SVG `stroke`.** `RW_STYLE.jump` uses `tint: "btn"` (`:11676`); `T.btn` is a `linear-gradient(...)` (`:45`); `SVGIcon` renders `stroke={color}` (`:5884`). Invalid → falls back to black. A category jump is a very common top signal, so the hero shows a black icon on the coloured panel, and in dark mode a black icon on a near-black card.

### What is clean here
Worth stating, because it narrows where to look: **there are no stuck-screen bugs in Richard Watch** — the engine is fully synchronous, no `setLoading` exists in the entire UI block. `subsumedBy` prefix slicing is off-by-one-safe. `detectBudgetPace` floors early-month projection (`:11519`). `forecastUpcoming` loops are guard-bounded. `PaceCard`'s RTL progress marker is correct in both directions. `richardWatch.totals` deliberately reports the max rather than a sum, with a comment explaining why summing would triple-count — correct reasoning.

## 4. The hero rewrite that just shipped

I reviewed this as uncommitted work; it landed in `88fc56d` mid-audit. Everything below is **live in master now**, which upgrades §4.1 from "catch before commit" to "ship a fix." Line numbers re-pinned to the new HEAD.

### 4.1 "Safe to spend" reads $0.00 in two taps — CRITICAL, live
`:9650-9653`:
```js
var heroCapRows    = budgetRows.filter(function(r) { return r.dir === "cap" && r.limit > 0; });
var heroBudgetRoom = round2(heroCapRows.reduce(function(s, r) { return s + Math.max(0, r.limit - r.spent); }, 0));
var safeToSpend    = Math.max(0, heroCapRows.length ? Math.min(heroCashRoom, heroBudgetRoom) : heroCashRoom);
```
`r.spent` comes from `spentInCat`, scoped by `inTimeframe(t, timeframe)` (`:9518`) — the header period badge the user can set to week/month/year/all. `r.limit` is a **monthly** cap. The subtraction mixes two windows.

Set the header to **Year** or **All** and `limit - spent` goes negative for every category → `heroBudgetRoom = 0` → `safeToSpend = min(cashRoom, 0) = 0`. The hero reads **`Safe to spend $0.00`, `Per day $0.00`, in red**. Two taps from default, no error. This is the single number the redesign is built around, and the panel it replaced was timeframe-independent — so the top-line number went from always-correct to corruptible by an unrelated toggle.

### 4.2 A panel labelled "This month" showing year figures — HIGH
`:10200` hardcodes `"This month"`; `income`/`expense` beneath it follow `timeframe` (`:9514`); `heroDaysLeft` is always calendar-month. On timeframe=Year: *"This month — Income ₪84,000 · Spent ₪61,000 · Days left 12."* The old panel carried `tfLabel`; the rewrite dropped it.

### 4.3 The eye toggle no longer hides your balance — MEDIUM, privacy
The blur wrapper (`filter: hidden ? "blur(11px)"`, `:10210`) covers only the `safeToSpend` span. `"Current balance"` and its value (`:10190`) sit in a sibling row *after* the `borderTop`, outside the blur. Tapping the eye to hide your money over someone's shoulder leaves the actual balance legible.

### 4.4 The hero is hardcoded English — HIGH
Across the four hero panels (`:10155–10285`) there is **exactly one** `tr()` call. Everything else is a literal: `"Safe to spend"`, `"Next 7 days"`, `"Per day"`, `"Current balance"`, `"This month"`, `"Income"/"Spent"/"Days left"`, `"Richard's next move"`, `"Money watcher"`, `"On track"/"Needs attention"`. The old panels used `tr("balance")`, `tr("income")`, `tr("spent")`. This is the first screen of the app, and it just lost its translations.

### 4.5 Smaller
- **Dead red branch:** `heroMonthPct > 100` (`:10209`) is unreachable — clamped with `Math.min(100, …)` at `:9628`. A user who spent 140% of income never sees the red bar.
- **Net worth removed from the hero** with no replacement; `netWorth`, `savTotal`, `bizTotal`, `invTotal` now feed only dead code (`heroVal`, `:9642`). Confirm that's intended scope.
- **~330 lines of Overview are now unreachable**: `trendChart()`, `donutChart()`, `ringChart()`, `rangeRow()`, the whole scrub apparatus, `tfLabel`, `merchants`, `winCats`.
- **`richardWatch()` now runs twice per Overview render, on every animation frame.** A new unmemoized call at `:9605` joins the one in `DailyBriefBanner` (`:8385`). Each runs `findMoney()` over the full history plus `forecastUpcoming()`. Overview re-renders once per rAF for 850ms (`:9672`) and once per `pointermove` during a hero drag (`:9738`). `grep -c useMemo` → **2** in 33,734 lines.

### 4.6 The model migration IS clean — no action needed
Verified end to end. `AI_MODEL_CORE = "claude-sonnet-5"` (`:16494`) and `AI_MODEL_FAST = "claude-haiku-4-5"` (`:16495`) are the **only two** model literals in the client; both are in `ALLOWED_MODELS`. `callClaude` now defaults to `model || AI_MODEL_CORE` instead of `undefined`. No call site sends an unmapped string. `maxTokens` is under the 2000 server cap everywhere (largest 1200). `thinking: {type:"disabled"}` is valid for Sonnet 5 and correctly gated so it never reaches Haiku.

One note: **the Haiku downgrade fails silently.** `buildRoadmap`, `runInvestingBasics`, `runStockTake` and `runWeeklyReview` all moved to Haiku and all catch a JSON parse failure by calling a deterministic local template. If Haiku's adherence is worse at these shapes, users quietly get the canned non-AI version and nothing logs or looks different. Add a `source: "ai" | "local"` counter — right now the downgrade is unmeasurable by construction.

This part of `88fc56d` is fine as shipped. It was the one thing in that commit that didn't need a second pass.

## 5. Dead ends and wrong numbers across the rest of the app

### 5.1 Sign Out discards unsaved data — and cancels the retry while hiding the error — HIGH
`handleLogout` (`:32828`) cancels the debounced save timer and the failure-retry timer, then calls `setSaveError("")`, then signs out. `save()` debounces the Firestore write by 800ms (`:32713`); `flushSave` schedules an 8-second retry on failure (`:32734`).

The comment explains the cancel exists so a late write can't resurrect a just-deleted account. But `handleLogout` is wired to **both** `onAccountDeleted` (`:33674`) *and* the everyday Profile "Sign out" (`:33667`). So the deletion-only safeguard is applied to the normal path.

Worst case: the red save-failure banner is on screen — the app is actively telling the user their data did not save — and Sign Out cancels the pending retry, clears the warning, and signs out. The edits are gone. There is also no confirmation on Sign Out at all. Fix: `flushSave()` before the cancel, or gate the cancel behind a deletion-only flag.

### 5.2 Changing currency relabels every historical amount without converting — HIGH
`function onSaveCurrency(sym) { _currency.sym = sym; setCurrency(sym); save({ currency: sym }); }` (`:33165`)

One tap in `CurrencyView` (`:28758`), no confirm. Only the symbol string swaps. $5,000 of savings instantly reads ¥5,000 across Overview, Goals, Trips, Debts, Business, Investing **and Richard's prompt context** (`:7907`). An order-of-magnitude misstatement of net worth, presented with full confidence. Foreign transactions that stored `origAmount/origCur/rate` keep a rate computed against the *old* main currency, so they're wrong a second, independent way. Nothing warns or offers to convert.

(Also: `CurrencyView` matches on `cur === opt.sym` and many currencies share `$`, so after switching to CAD the checkmark also lights on USD/AUD/NZD.)

### 5.3 "Mark paid" on an invoice moves no money — HIGH
`toggleInvoicePaid` (`:26962`) flips a status string and nothing else. No revenue entry, no cash movement. So after marking a $5,000 invoice paid:
- the "Unpaid" stat drops to $0 and the row strikes through,
- `businessCash` is unchanged,
- the **Tax pot** (`:27812`) stays $0 on $5,000 collected, because `bizQuarterRevenue` (`:21143`) only sums entries with `e.revenue`,
- P&L still shows the business as loss-making.

The CFO screen simultaneously shows "all caught up" on invoices and zero revenue. Recording it properly requires *also* finding "Log revenue" and re-entering the same $5,000 — and nothing on screen says which to do.

### 5.4 "Cancel these 3" cancels nothing, and taking the advice makes the advice vanish — HIGH
`cancelFindings` (`:12357`) only appends ids to `foundMoney.dismissed`. No draft letter, no reminder, no transaction, no flag — despite button copy that promises cancellation (`"Cancel these " + pickedIds.length`, `:12501`).

Then `richardWatch` filters findings by that same `dismissed` list (`:11764`) *before* running `linkLeaksToGap` (`:11814`). So the moment the user follows the app's own recommendation, `plan.picked` empties, `plan.covers` flips false, and the goal card stops offering a plan at all.

### 5.5 Dismissing a leak is permanent; the "Recovered" tally mixes units — MED-HIGH
- **`dismissed` is append-only.** `.concat` at `:8463` and `:12324`, and **no removal path anywhere** except full account reset (`:32689`). The ghost button ("Keep it" / "Looks fine") sits beside the primary action, one tap, no confirm. Mis-tap it on a real $40/mo forgotten subscription and Richy can never show it again.
- **`creditOf(f)`** (`:8488`) returns `f.annual > 0 ? f.annual : f.amount`. So one fee row credits `meta.total` via one button (`:8614`) and `f.annual` via another (`:8629`) — two different numbers into the same running total. `duplicate` findings carry `annual: 0` (`:11087`) and credit a one-off; `recurring`/`hike`/`renewal` credit a full year. Both land in "Recovered so far", rendered directly beside the strictly-annual "Recoverable / year" (`:8572-8578`) with no unit distinction. The tally also gates a badge (`m-02-treasure-hunter`, `tally ≥ 500`, `:3014`), so mixed units inflate progression. `resolve` only ever adds — there is no way to correct it down.

### 5.6 Bank Sync "Send a test transaction" can hang in "Sending…" forever — MEDIUM
`sendTest` exists twice (`:29693`, `:30024`). Neither has an `AbortController` or a timeout, and both open with `if (testState === "sending") return`. Once the flag sticks, the button is permanently inert — label frozen on "Sending…", `disabled` set — and the only escape is leaving and re-entering the screen.

This is the exact failure the file documents and defends against elsewhere: `callClaude` (`:16498-16525`) builds a 55s timeout + AbortController precisely because *"a fetch that stalls without erroring (backgrounded tab, cell handoff, captive portal) never settles on its own, which would leave the caller's loading state stuck on forever."* That protection was never applied here.

### 5.7 A trip is deleted instantly and totally — while one line item inside it asks twice — HIGH
`removeTrip` (`:15763`) is called directly from the button (`:16401`). One tap destroys the trip, every budget bucket, every logged expense and the linked balance transaction. No confirm, no undo, no toast.

The same screen guards *smaller* actions far more carefully: deleting a single expense gets an inline confirm (`:16337`); deleting a single bucket gets a full "This cannot be undone" dialog (`:16465`). "Delete trip" sits ~60px below "End trip" (`:16395`), which is the reversible one — **the destructive button is the unguarded one.**

Related: **"Resplit" silently deletes hand-made budget buckets.** `mapAllocations` (`:15543`) rebuilds from the fixed `TRIP_CATEGORIES` table, so every `custom_*` bucket vanishes. The failure path does the same. `applyAllocToWizard` (`:15532`) preserves them correctly — the two Richard paths disagree with each other.

### 5.8 The onboarding questionnaire has a one-way door — MED-HIGH
`advance()` (`:8075`) calls `setPhase("story")`, and `setPhase("q")` appears **nowhere**. `MathStoryScreen` (`:7683-7812`) renders three sub-phases and has **no back button and no exit** at any of them. Type monthly income as 5000 instead of 500, tap "Do the math", and the only way out is to complete the flow with the wrong numbers — which then seed the generated plan, the suggested budgets and Richard's permanent user context. This is a *distinct* dead end from the known Redo-Questionnaire trap, and it's in first-run.

### 5.9 Medium and low
- **Debt payoff inputs are never persisted** (`:21531`). "Extra per month" and snowball/avalanche are plain `useState`; `onSaveDebts` only persists the debts array (`:21493`). Reopen the screen and the "Debt-free by" date silently jumps years later. The user's plan looks thrown away.
- **Nine badges and a rank gate are permanently unearnable.** Nothing ever writes `debt.cleared` or `note.settled` — the debt record (`:21491`) has no `cleared` field, and `doSettle` (`:13716`) *removes* the note rather than flagging it. So `debtsCleared` and `notesSettled` (`:3237`, `:3251`) are structurally always 0, killing `i-04`, `i-05`, `i-07`, `l-02`, `l-03/04/05`, `l-07`, and the debt half of the **"Debt Breaker"** rank at level 31 (`:2859`). `i-06-avalanche` has no trigger-map entry at all. There is also no "mark this debt paid off" action anywhere.
- **A reminder set while notifications are blocked is saved, then silently consumed.** `applyReminder` (`:13765`) saves regardless of `granted` and promises *"the note will still show a due badge."* But `fireDue` (`:32976`) marks it `fired: true` (`:32917`) even though `fireReminder` bails on the first line when permission isn't granted (`:13540`) — and `overdue` requires `!fired` (`:13893`). The user gets neither the alert nor the badge.
- **The invoice trash icon deletes with no confirm** (`:28553`), a 22px target ~8px from "Mark paid" — while the same component guards closing an account, deleting an account, and deleting a capital entry.
- **The Leumi demo error renders behind the modal.** `approve()` (`:29799`) resets `busy` but deliberately doesn't close, so the `zIndex: 500` modal stays up while `setErr` writes the message into the card *underneath* it (`:29884`). The button silently flips back with no explanation, and reopening wipes the message (`:29886`) before it can be read.
- **"Copy message" on the cancellation letter is a silent no-op** where `navigator.clipboard` is missing (`copyDraft`, `:8523`) — no fallback, no error, label unchanged. The unawaited `writeText` also flips the label to "Copied" on rejection. `copy(which)` in BankSyncJourney (`:29614`) solves this correctly 20,000 lines later.
- **`DebtView`'s delete-confirm is sticky with no cancel** — `setDelId(null)` appears only inside `doDelete` (`:21496`).

### What is clean here
**Trip "End trip" is genuinely reversible** — the round trip through `TripHistoryView` and `onOpenTrip` works. **`tr("deductExplain")` is honest** — the copy matches `reserveTrip`/`syncTripTx`. **Deleting a trip bucket with spend in it** offers a correct "move into another bucket" merge first. **Business account deletion copy is accurate** — "close" returns the cash and says so, "delete" loses it and says so. **Account deletion (`PrivacyView`, `:30132`)** requires typing DELETE, offers export inline, handles `partial` responses, and resets `delBusy` on every path — genuinely well built. **Leumi disconnect really does remove the demo rows** (field names match). **The onboarding plan-generation blocker has a visible "continue without waiting" escape**, deliberately.

## 6. Richard bypasses his own guardrail in two more places

The 30 Aug audit found one advice surface skipping the server `GUARDRAIL` and drew the right lesson: *"any future guardrail needs to sit at the point financial strings are rendered, not at the API boundary."* Here is the proof, twice.

**6.1 Every API failure turns Richard into a US financial-advice bot — HIGH, legal.** `:18500`: `var response = err || !text ? Richard(msg) : text;`. On any network error, Richard falls back to a hardcoded local bank (`:17630`) that is deterministic client code and **never sees `GUARDRAIL`**. ## 8. What I think the right move is

**The execution problem is now solved — the four fixes landed.** What's left is a *sequencing* problem: `88fc56d` shipped those fixes and a 400-line hero rewrite in one commit, and the rewrite carried §4.1 in with it. `ROADMAP.md` already holds a good plan and does not need rewriting. It needs the one thing it has never had: **a gate that something other than intention enforces.**

### The single change I would make first — and it is not a bug fix

**Add a pre-commit check that fails the build on the things we keep regressing:**

1. `T.ink3` must equal the passing hex — **locks in the fix you just made** so it can't drift back the way it went 661 → 693 → 811.
2. Any new `setX(true)` busy flag must have a matching `.finally(`.
3. No new `toISOString()` date site (currently 67).
4. Every `currentTab` value must resolve to a dictionary key — **would have caught `watchBrief` before it shipped.**

That is one afternoon, and it's what stops this list regrowing while you work through it.

### Ordering, against 21 days

**Day 1 — the criticals the last commit left behind.** §4.1 (`safeToSpend` scoped to the month — this is live and it's your headline number). §5.1 (`flushSave()` before the logout cancel). The four Watch dictionary keys (kills the `watchBrief` header). All small; together the highest-value day available.

**Days 3–4 — decide Richard Watch, deliberately.** Either wire up `.actions` (the code is already written — it just needs reading), translate the four screens, and fix §3.5 and §3.6; **or hide the Watch entry points behind a flag for v1** and ship it in 1.1.

**My recommendation: hide it.** Three days is not enough to finish it properly, it sits on the hero where the App Store reviewer will tap it first, and right now it is the single largest source of the exact complaints being escalated. Shipping it as-is is the worst of the three options.

**Days 5–8 — reversibility, as one pattern.** The Undo-toast recommendation from 30 Aug still stands and is still strictly better than adding confirmation dialogs. Apply it once, everywhere: trip delete (§5.7), invoice delete, `foundMoney.dismissed` (§5.5), goal contributions, stock delete, the two chevron rows (§3.6). Add the `save()` empty-array guard, which closes the household wipe and the stale closure together. Add a confirm-and-convert step to the currency change (§5.2) — that one is a data-integrity issue, not a UX nicety.

**Days 9–11 — stuck screens and dates.** One `localToday()`/`localMonth()` helper for all 67 UTC sites. `.finally()` on all 13 busy flags. AbortController on both `sendTest` copies. Triage the 51 empty catches — most can take the existing `saveError` banner.

**Days 12–16 — submission blockers.** Accessibility sweep. `theme-color` consistency. The onboarding USD carousel. The advice-surface decision — §6.1 is the easy half: **delete** the Roth/401k/index-fund entries from the local bank. They are unlicensed advice on an unmonitored path, so that is a deletion, not a rewrite. Legal doc dates.

**Buffer to 23 Sep.** Re-run the nightly simulation and compare dropout against the 19 Aug baseline.

**One sequencing ask, given how §4.1 got in:** keep fixes and features in separate commits from here to submission. The four TIER 0 fixes in `88fc56d` were correct and reviewable; the hero rewrite bundled with them was neither, and it is the reason there is a new CRITICAL on this page. A drop-in reporter is under a day.
